A gift is scored from the donor's own energy 100 ticks later. An attack is scored only if that attacker eats the corpse, or reaches the spot after it is gone. A corpse never reached adds nothing. These scores are not inherited and do not change synapses.

Assumptions

Energy is an artificial budget. Food reappears in a new random place after it is eaten. Plant meals extend lifespan; scavenged meals only restore energy. Seasons change plant growth. These are engineered behaviors, not fly physiology. Offspring inherit and mutate speed, lifespan, fertility, metabolism, and decoder gains, plus a few nearly useless quirks. Caution, generosity, and aggression are inherited scales for hazard avoidance, gifts, and attacks. They are tendencies, not character traits. MaleCNS topology does not change.
